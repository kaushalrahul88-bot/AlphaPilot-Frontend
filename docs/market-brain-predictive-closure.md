# Market Brain Predictive Research Closure

Market Brain predictive research v3 through v5 is closed with **NO REPLICATED EDGE**. Market Brain remains a descriptive research/context layer only. Its breadth, sector, cross-asset, news and official-signal outputs do not authorize, veto, score or modify production trades.

Any future predictive Market Brain experiment requires a genuinely new pre-specified hypothesis and timestamp-aligned historical data. Existing v3-v5 thresholds must not be loosened to rescue prior results.
